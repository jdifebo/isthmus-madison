# Isthmus

Make a life for yourself in downtown Madison, Wisconsin, or explore on foot, by car, in the air, and aboard Metro Rapid. [Play on GitHub Pages](https://jdifebo.github.io/isthmus-madison/) or open the standalone `Isthmus.html` in a WebGL-enabled desktop browser.

## Story mode

Choose **Start the story** to begin *A Place of Your Own*. You start outside the University Book Store with a volunteer shift to pick up. Follow the tracked objective, press **E** to interact on foot, and check the **Journal** with **J**. The **I** shortcut opens your inventory.

- Three main quests lead to your first property. Organize a book swap in **Open House** after you move in. Side quests, repeatable deliveries, and City Dispatch shifts let you earn cash along the way.
- Your inventory carries quest items, souvenirs, keys, and the in-game photos you take during the postcard quest.
- Buy Bassett Studio or East Wash Workshop with in-game earnings. Return to an owned property from the journal. The studio completes the chapter and the workshop increases delivery pay.
- Progress saves in your current browser. **Continue story** returns you to your most recent story interaction. Free roam and Saturday Market are also available from the title screen.
- Your car stays where you leave it. Press **2** to find it, then press **E** beside it to get in. Brake and press **E** to get out. On mission pickups and completions, a car left far away can be moved to an available curb space nearby.
- Press **M** or **Esc** for the full-screen pause map. Choose an objective, place, or bus stop for a walking or driving route. Switch the minimap between **North up** and **Heading up** with its orientation button. Free roam still lets you jump directly to landmarks.

The current chapter uses fictional characters, events, and purchasable properties. The University Book Store and Fromagination have handcrafted storefronts at their mapped locations. Memorial Union Terrace has sunburst chairs and sailboats on Lake Mendota. [Start the story](https://jdifebo.github.io/isthmus-madison/?story=1) · [University Book Store exterior](https://strategiccommunication.wisc.edu/contact-us/) · [Fromagination storefront design](https://www.cricketdesignworks.com/portfolio/fromagination) · [Wisconsin Union Terrace](https://union.wisc.edu/visit/terrace-at-the-memorial-union)

### City Dispatch and community projects

Open **Journal → Jobs** to see five City Dispatch shifts. The closest first pickup is **Soundcheck Supplies** at the University Book Store. Busy crowds and platform queues can add $14 in rush pay. The pickup quote stays with the shift until you deliver. After two Dispatch shifts, you can survey Route A to Blair. Express counts for the survey, and riding the full trip pays a $12 bonus.

Handing over supplies draws people to the gathering or brings forward its next start. The Blair survey gives walk requests temporary priority. Completing different shifts unlocks projects you can fund with in-game cash. The Library Mall noticeboard brings sets around sooner, the State Street open mic supports longer sets and larger crowds, and the Blair crossing fund gives walk requests lasting priority. Funded projects carry over when you return to your story.

After **A First Shift**, pick up **City Pulse** at Capitol Square. Record a live crowd at the State Street set and watch a walk phase at Blair before filing your notes at Breese Stevens Field.

## City Watch

Sidewalk sets on State Street and Library Mall, a sketch circle on Capitol Square, and a meetup near Breese Stevens bring people together. Some listeners head to Route A when a set ends. A busy stop holds the bus longer while riders board. At Blair and Paterson, people getting off can gather at the crosswalk and trigger a walk phase that backs up traffic. Some riders set off on foot when the wait gets long. The simulated activities and transit run while you explore.

Press **L** for City Watch and its activity feed. Turn on **Sound** there to hear nearby guitar, bus, and crossing sounds. On foot, press **E** near a performer to ask for an encore, or at a marked crossing to request a walk signal. The **City activity** list on the pause map shows where things are happening and lets you set a route there. All events and timing are fictional. [Watch State Street](https://jdifebo.github.io/isthmus-madison/?watch=state) · [Watch near Breese Stevens](https://jdifebo.github.io/isthmus-madison/?watch=paterson)

## Controls

| Action | Input |
| --- | --- |
| Walk, fly, or steer | WASD or arrow keys |
| Look around on foot or in flight | Drag the scene |
| Ascend / descend | Space / C |
| Sprint or boost in flight | Shift |
| Brake in the car | Space |
| Walk / drive or find your car / fly | 1 / 2 / 3 |
| Interact, browse a stall, or board or exit the bus | E near an interaction or on the bus |
| Open the journal or inventory in story mode | J / I |
| Take off / land | F |
| Open the pause map | M or Esc |
| Hide or restore the HUD | H |
| Open City Watch | L |
| Return to your current starting point | R |
| Close a panel or resume play | Esc |

The playable map covers about 4.1 by 2.6 km, from the downtown edge of the UW campus to the near east side. Take flight over Lake Monona or jump to a landmark from the field map. Flight keeps the camera clear of terrain and rooftops. Switching to a ground mode over open water returns you to your last ground position.

Public aerial photos cover the terrain and surrounding landscape. Procedural clouds, water, façade materials and swaying tree crowns respond to daylight, golden hour, and blue hour. Most building heights are estimated, and the Capitol and Monona Terrace have custom exteriors. The procedural atmosphere takes inspiration from [Abyssal Garden](https://github.com/Noniv/abyssal-garden).

Street trees vary in height and canopy shape using the City Forestry inventory. From ground level you can see layered leaves, grass, flowerbeds around Capitol Square, and textured pavement. Pedestrians stroll along State Street in both directions and wait for signals at its modeled crossings.

Articulated Route A buses run between East Campus and Baldwin. Eastbound buses follow Johnson, State, Carroll, Main, and Pinckney to East Washington. Westbound buses return via Pinckney, Mifflin, State, Gorham, and University. Cars use the general lanes on East Washington and brake for traffic lights, queues, and the player. The field map tracks the simulated buses, which arrive more often than the real service. [Metro's Route A directions](https://www.cityofmadison.com/metro/routes-schedules/route-a/turn-by-turn)

Open the game with `?stop=paterson` to start at the station. The same shortcut works for Blair and Baldwin. [Start at Paterson](https://jdifebo.github.io/isthmus-madison/?stop=paterson)

Choose **Ride Metro Rapid** at the title screen to start at the eastbound State Street shelter. Press **E** at any departing platform to summon a bus or board one already there. Drag to look around, press **E** to request the next stop, or choose **Express** to instantly reach a stop ahead. Both ride options count for the **Take the A** side quest. The playable ride ends at Baldwin eastbound and East Campus westbound. [Start at the eastbound State Street shelter](https://jdifebo.github.io/isthmus-madison/?stop=state-east&platform=1)

Choose **Saturday Market** to walk Capitol Square among shoppers and browse produce, flowers, bread, and cheese at fictional stalls. Use **Regular day** to return to the usual scene. On market days, simulated Route A buses use a detour based on the city's Outer Loop, with temporary Square stops on East Doty and West Dayton. [Enter Saturday Market](https://jdifebo.github.io/isthmus-madison/?market=1) · [Dane County Farmers’ Market](https://dcfm.org/index.php?p=markets%2Fsaturday-on-the-square) · [City street closures](https://www.cityofmadison.com/news/2026-04-07/street-closures-for-dane-county-farmers-market-on-the-square) · [Metro's downtown routing study](https://www.cityofmadison.com/metro/documents/brt/downtownrouting.pdf)

The field map also jumps to Orton Park and Breese Stevens Field. Nearby homes use mapped footprints and parcel boundaries, with generated roof shapes, siding, windows, doors, porches, paths, driveways, and planting beds. The house styles draw from the [Third Lake Ridge district](https://www.cityofmadison.com/dpced/planning/historic-preservation/local-historic-districts/third-lake-ridge) and [Marquette Bungalows](https://www.cityofmadison.com/dpced/planning/historic-preservation/local-historic-districts/marquette-bungalows). Breese Stevens Field has a lined soccer pitch, stands, and a stone arcade inspired by its [historic grandstand](https://www.cityofmadison.com/parks/find-a-park/breese-stevens/history).

## Data and source

- [City of Madison open data](https://data-cityofmadison.opendata.arcgis.com/) and [Greater Madison MPO](https://www.cityofmadison.com/mpo/maps-data/data-resources) for parks, water, street trees and supplementary roads.
- [City Forestry street tree inventory](https://maps.cityofmadison.com/arcgis/rest/services/Public/OPEN_DATA/MapServer/0) for tree locations, species groups, and trunk diameters.
- [City of Madison parcel geometry](https://maps.cityofmadison.com/arcgis/rest/services/Public/OPEN_DATA2/MapServer/0) for house lot boundaries used to place paths and driveways.
- [Greater Madison MPO transportation layers](https://maps.cityofmadison.com/arcgis/rest/services/MPO/RTP_PIM_Board/MapServer) and [Madison Metro bus-only lane guide](https://www.cityofmadison.com/metro/routes-schedules/bus-rapid-transit/bus-only-traffic-lanes) for the East Washington Avenue stations, lanes, and traffic signal locations.
- [Metro's downtown transit design](https://www.cityofmadison.com/metro/documents/brt/2020-12-22LPARevisionMemo.pdf) and [State Street platform locations](https://www.cityofmadison.com/metro/documents/brt/environmental/MadisonBRT_DCE_AppendixF_VisualQualityTechnicalReport.pdf) for curb stops and the alignment through Capitol Square.
- [Dane County, City of Madison and Ayres Associates](https://services1.arcgis.com/Ezk9fcjSUkeadg6u/ArcGIS/rest/services/3DMadison_3D_WFL1/FeatureServer/0) for building footprints.
- [OpenStreetMap contributors](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) for street and path centerlines and some building heights. The OpenStreetMap-derived data is available under the [Open Database License](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/). The processed geodata is in `data/madison.js`. The download and conversion scripts reproduce it.
- [Mapzen / AWS Terrain Tiles](https://registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles/) for elevation.
- [USGS / USDA NAIP](https://www.usgs.gov/centers/eros/science/usgs-eros-archive-aerial-photography-national-agriculture-imagery-program-naip) for the downtown and regional aerial textures.
- [Wisconsin Department of Administration](https://doa.wi.gov/Pages/CapitolTours/Wisconsin-State-Capitol-Exterior.aspx) and [Monona Terrace](https://www.mononaterrace.com/about-us/) for landmark references.
- [Three.js](https://threejs.org/) for the WebGL renderer. Its license is included in `assets/THREE-LICENSE.txt` and in the standalone HTML.

Open `index.html` to use the individual source files, or run `node scripts/serve.mjs` for a local preview. Run `node scripts/package.mjs` after source changes to rebuild the standalone file. Refreshing the data requires network access. Run `node scripts/download-map.mjs` and `node scripts/build-map.mjs` before packaging. Downloads are cached in `.cache/`.
